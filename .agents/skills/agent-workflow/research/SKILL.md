# Research

## When to use

- The answer depends on source code, tests, issue history, or first-party documentation.
- A fact is niche, unstable, or important enough that memory is not sufficient.
- A design decision needs a durable evidence record.

## How to use

1. State the question, scope, and decision it supports.
2. Search local sources before broadening the investigation.
3. Prefer primary documentation, source code, tests, and issue history.
4. Separate observed facts, inferences, guesses, and unresolved gaps.
5. Stop when evidence is sufficient for the decision.
6. Record source locations and the next experiment when evidence is incomplete.

## Boundaries

- Do not turn an unverified claim into a requirement.
- Do not copy external product text into project rules.
- Do not expand research into implementation without a routed decision.
